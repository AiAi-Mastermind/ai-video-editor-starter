# Sample Brand Kit

This practice brand is fictional.

## Business

- Business name: Sunrise Family Bakery
- Owner: Dana
- What it sells: Custom cakes and weekday breakfast boxes
- Audience: Busy parents within 10 miles
- Main call to action: Order this week's breakfast box at the link

## Look and voice

- Primary color: `#F4A261`
- Secondary color: `#264653`
- Font: Use a simple default font
- Tone: Warm, quick, a little funny
- Never appear: Real customer names

## Video defaults

- Output sizes: 16:9 and 9:16.
- Captions: white text on a dark rounded background, in the bottom third, with no more than 6 words per line.
- Hook: big text for the first 3 seconds.
- End card: business name and call to action for 4 seconds.
- Voice ID: in `.env`
- Avatar ID: in `.env`

- Background music: none

Music options: none, light, upbeat.

## Delivery

- Pace: fast
- Energy: upbeat
- Pronunciation: say the name as written
